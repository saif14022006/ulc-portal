ULC Toolkit is the campus companion for University Law College, Quetta — built for students and teachers who need clean, print-ready documents and classroom tools in one place.

Independent toolkit (not affiliated with ULC or the University of Balochistan). Designed to solve real student and teacher problems.

STUDENT TOOLS (many work without login)
• Assignment Cover Page — multiple professional templates, download as PDF
• Application / Letter Generator — leave, rechecking, apology, fee issues, character certificate, hostel leave, migration, ID card, and general applications
• LLB & LLM Syllabus — browse HEC five-year LLB and LL.M. courses
• Award List — quizzes, assignments, mid & final using the official ULC formula
• Admission Aggregate — Matric · Intermediate · LAT merit calculator
• GPA Tracker — course marks, semester GPA, and CGPA
• Timetable — keep your weekly class schedule on your phone

STUDENT ACCOUNT
• Personal dashboard with profile and photo
• Semester records and subject marks
• Transcript PDF ready to print or share
• My Files — saved covers, applications, and documents
• Cloud sync when signed in

TEACHER / FACULTY DESK
• Manage classes by subject and semester
• Import student roster from PDF or photo (OCR), or add manually
• Session roster shares roll number and name for classroom coordination
• Attendance tracking with PDF export
• Marks entry for quizzes, assignments, mid and final
• Award list PDF and Excel export
• Marks and attendance stay private in your teacher workspace

WHY ULC TOOLKIT
• Mobile-friendly and fast
• Print-ready PDFs for college submission
• Free tools without an account; sign in to save and sync
• Built for law college workflows in Quetta

Download ULC Toolkit and get campus paperwork done faster — from covers and applications to award lists and teacher records.
